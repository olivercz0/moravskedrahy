CERS V4 Network

CERS V4 brings into one game many routes, such as: electrified single, double tracks, unelectrified single, double tracks, freight only routes, all in one pretty much!

Electrified routes: (red color)

They are marked on the map in the color red, from Klagenfurt all the way to Ujezdec u Luhacovic, throughout Brandys nad Orlici, Veseli nad Moravou, Vlarsky Prusmyk, Bylnice, Mohelnice, Kojetin, and Kyjov. It goes from Austria, all the way to Moravia, cool, right? with a few stations, and some stops, and also a good amount of industrial tracks, perfect for freight!

UNelectrified routes: (blue color)

Marked as blue on the map, there are more of these, variously scattered, the main one is from Bzenec to Mohelnice, through Brniste, or from Mohelnice all the way to Kouty nad Desnou / Zegiestow, through Bludov, Veseli nad Moravou, Ostruzna, Celechovice na Hane and Kostelec na hane, where the route splits towards Poland (Zegiestow), and Kouty nad Desnou.

Industrial tracks: (black color)

Marked as black on the map, there are a good number of these, in Kyjov, Bohuslavice u Kyjova, Mohelnice, Bludov, Bylnice, Vlarsky Prusmyk + Slavicin, Veseli nad Moravou, Ostruzna, Jivova (more like between Jivova and Ostruzna), Celechovice na Hane, Kostelec na Hane and Cejetice, all these locations have various purposes, for containers, fuel, wood, coal, whatever!

The map is MAINLY 3 kV DC, however, from Brandys nad Orlici towards Hessendorf, Klagenfurt, simply said, Austria, is 15 kV AC at 16.7 Hz, so if you wanna be realistic, change locomotives somewhere.

The game is 2 years old next year, so there might be a smaller event, however, right now, there is a winter update AND event planned! every week, you will unlock a specific loco or something, if you manage to drive the weekly goal.
